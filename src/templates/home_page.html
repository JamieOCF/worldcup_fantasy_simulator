The details

Everything you need to know.

Squad locked on day one

Unlike traditional fantasy, there are no weekly transfers or captain swaps. You pick your 25-man squad before the tournament starts and that's it — the same players carry you from the group stage all the way to the final. Every choice matters from the very beginning.

11 starters · 14 on the bench · Max 5 substitutions

Points accumulation

Goals, assists, clean sheets, saves, and match ratings all contribute to your tally. The deeper your run, the more matches your players appear in — and the more points you can rack up.

Progression over points

Your runs are ranked by round reached first, then points scored. A semi-final exit always outranks a quarter-final exit, no matter the score. Go further, not just harder.

Your run history

Every attempt is stored. See all your past squads, how far they went, and how many points they accumulated. Track your improvement and figure out what it takes to go one round further.

No opponents. No pressure.

This is purely single-player. You're not competing against other people — you're competing against the tournament, and against yourself. Play at your own pace, experiment with different squads, and see what works.

The tournament

June - July 2026

FIFA World Cup 2026 is the largest edition in history — 48 teams, three host nations, 104 matches. The expanded format means more rounds, more drama, and more chances for your squad to rack up points. Every extra game your team survives is a bigger tally.
